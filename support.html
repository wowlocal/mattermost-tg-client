Relay: Team Chat for iPhone and iPad

Getting started

Relay connects to your organization's Mattermost server. Open the app, enter your server's address (for example chat.example.com) and sign in with the same account you use on the web: username or email and password, multi-factor authentication or single sign-on, as your server allows. You can add more servers and accounts in Settings.

Requirements

  • iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.
  • A Mattermost server reachable over HTTPS. Relay is tested with Mattermost Server 11 (ESR) and 12.

Push notifications

Notifications are sent by your Mattermost server. For Relay to receive them, your server's administrator has to point the server's push notification setting at a push proxy that serves Relay. If you don't get notifications, ask your administrator; the app works without them and shows new messages whenever it is open.

Common questions

  • I can't sign in. Check that the server address opens in Safari and that it starts with https://. If your organization uses single sign-on, choose it on the sign-in screen.
  • A message didn't send. Messages sent while offline are kept and sent when the connection returns; a failed message can be resent from its menu.
  • How do I remove my data? Log out of the account in Settings, or delete the app. Data on the server is managed by your server's administrator.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question at github.com/wowlocal/mattermost-tg-client/issues. Please don't post passwords, server addresses you want to keep private, or message contents.
