Privacy Policy

Relay: Team Chat for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 27, 2026

Relay is a client for Mattermost servers. It has no servers or accounts of its own, and the developer of Relay does not collect any data about you or your use of the app.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services are built into the app.
  • Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer of Relay.
  • Your data is not sold, shared with data brokers or used to track you across apps and websites.

Where your data goes

Everything the app sends — your sign-in, messages, files, voice messages, reactions and profile changes — goes only to the Mattermost server you sign in to. That server is run by you or by your organization, and its operator's privacy policy applies to the data stored there. Link previews and images are loaded through your server, not directly from the linked websites.

Push notifications

If your server's administrator turns on push notifications for Relay, your server sends each notification through the push notification proxy configured on the server, which passes it to the Apple Push Notification service. Depending on the server's settings, a notification can contain the sender, the channel name and the message text, or only identifiers that the app then uses to load the message from your server. A push proxy operated by the developer of Relay only forwards notifications: it does not store their contents or your device token.

Data on your device

  • Messages and media are cached on your device in an encrypted database so the app can open quickly and work offline. Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Logging out of an account removes its data from the device; deleting the app removes everything.
  • You can protect the app with a passcode or Face ID in Settings → Privacy and Security.

Permissions

The app asks for the camera, photo library, microphone and Face ID only when you use a feature that needs them: taking or attaching photos and videos, recording voice messages, or unlocking the app. Photos, videos and recordings leave your device only when you send them, and only to your Mattermost server.

Diagnostics

The app can keep crash and performance reports that iOS provides, and an optional diagnostic log that you turn on yourself. They stay on your device. They leave it only if you export them (Settings → About Relay → Diagnostics) and choose where to send them; message text, passwords and tokens are never written to them. If you opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share crash reports with the developer; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Relay is a workplace communication tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
